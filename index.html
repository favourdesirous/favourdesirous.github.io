Crow Animate

A personal desktop tool for making short videos.

Crow Animate is a desktop app its owner uses to edit gameplay recordings and voice-overs into short vertical videos for their own channels, including the Project Lucent and Lucent Sky community.

When the owner approves a video in the app, it can upload that video to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, scheduled to publish at a chosen time. It does not access anyone else's account or data.
