Privacy policy

Crow Animate · last updated 10 October 2026

What the app does

Crow Animate is a desktop app used by its owner to upload short videos they have made and approved to their own YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API.

What data it uses

  • Your Google sign-in token. When you connect YouTube, Google gives the app a token that lets it upload videos to your channel. The token is stored encrypted on your own computer only. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Your channel's name and ID, read from YouTube so the app can show which channel is connected.
  • The videos you approve, with their titles and descriptions, which the app uploads to your channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or store anyone else's data.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It has no servers of its own; everything runs on your computer and talks directly to Google.

YouTube and Google

Crow Animate uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can disconnect YouTube in the app, which deletes the stored token, or revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
